Opportunity Engine

A private market research tool, built and used by one person.

What it does

Opportunity Engine helps its developer study software product ideas. For each idea, it estimates how many people search for it on Google, using the keyword planning data of the developer's own Google Ads account.

How it uses Google Ads

  • It reads keyword ideas and historical search volumes (Keyword Planner) for the developer's own Google Ads account.
  • It never creates, edits or runs campaigns, and never spends money.
  • It is not offered to the public. No other person signs in to it.
